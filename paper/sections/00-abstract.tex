\begin{abstract}
Evaluations of LLM-generated code focus on correctness rather than the energy the code
consumes when executed. We study energy efficiency along three factors: the generating LLM
(Claude, GPT, and Gemini via their official coding CLIs), the language (Python, Java,
C++), and the hardware platform. 630 programs for 14 tasks are checked for correctness and
measured under controlled conditions on an x86 laptop and an Arm Mac. Language dominates:
generated Python consumed about 13 times and Java twice the energy of C++. LLMs differ
less: in a mixed model, Claude's programs needed 31\,\% more energy than GPT's, while
Gemini's did not differ significantly; the gap stems mainly from algorithmic choices in a
few tasks. The LLM ranking did not depend on platform or language. Energy correlated
almost perfectly with execution time but hardly with peak memory.
\end{abstract}
