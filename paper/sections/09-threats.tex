\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

Energy interfaces differ in scope (\texttt{powermetrics} only estimates), so absolute
values are not compared across platforms. Hidden system prompts and effort levels make
\emph{LLM} a model--tool factor; we record versions and dates.
All tasks are public and may be memorised. Some C++ runs last below 0.1\,s, where start-up
weighs more. With two platforms, results may not generalise to other
hardware, models, languages, or real-world code.
