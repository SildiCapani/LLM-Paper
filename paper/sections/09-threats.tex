\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

Energy interfaces differ in scope (\texttt{powermetrics} only estimates), so absolute
values are not compared across platforms. Hidden system prompts and effort levels make
\emph{LLM} a model--tool factor; we record versions and dates.
All tasks are public and may be memorised. Solving an input repeatedly in one process
amortises one-time initialisation, and the 10\,s cap leaves some C++ runs below 0.1\,s,
where start-up weighs more. Results may not generalise to other models, languages, or
real-world code.
