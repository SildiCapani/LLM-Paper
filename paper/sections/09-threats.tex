\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\paragraph{Construct validity.}
Energy measurement methods differ in which components they capture (CPU package and
DRAM via RAPL vs.\ whole-board power via external meters), which limits the
comparability of absolute values across platforms.

\paragraph{External validity.}
Results are limited to the selected LLMs (and their versions at the time of the study),
tasks, three languages, and three platforms, and may not generalise to other models,
languages, or architectures, or to larger, real-world codebases.

\paragraph{Confounding of model and implementation.}
The effect of an LLM cannot be fully separated from the concrete algorithm it chooses;
these strategy differences are, however, part of the phenomenon under study.

\paragraph{Data contamination.}
The EffiBench-X tasks were released between November 2023 and October 2024 and the CLBG
tasks and their solutions have been public for many years; all studied LLMs were likely
trained on them. Memorised solutions may therefore inflate correctness and affect
efficiency. Since all LLMs are exposed to the same tasks, this threat primarily concerns
absolute results rather than the comparison between models; the contrast between
